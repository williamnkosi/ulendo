## Compatibility clarifications

1. Coordinate expiry preserves C02 replay safety: coordinate-bearing sample/projection expires at acceptedAt + 30 minutes, while lastSequence, generation high-water mark, one-way lastSampleFingerprint and original lastAck remain for current-session duplicate semantics. The fingerprint is a normalized-payload digest, not raw coordinates/token text; the ack carries identity/sequence/capture/receipt/deadline metadata, not coordinates/token. Exact latest duplicate after coordinate cleanup returns the original ack without refreshing freshness, changing token or republishing position. Changed/lower sequence and replacement/close retain original outcomes. Generation and online revision never reset.
2. R07's historical phrase “no tracking permission” means no use as a fresh position. Existing nonterminal participants may read/display a still-readable stale projection until conditional cleanup. Freshness does not change lifecycle or revoke participant authorization. Terminal state immediately revokes location reads even if deletion lags.
3. Ordinary history expires at endedAt + 90 days. Enforce query/read expiry even before asynchronous physical deletion; cursor lifetime cannot extend it. Late/retried archive work cannot insert/resurrect expired ordinary history. A previously acknowledged terminal archive permits identity-conditional cleanup without recreating it. A never-acknowledged sole live terminal snapshot remains R02's explicit monitored safety exception with unfinished work retained: do not fabricate acknowledgement, delete sole recovery evidence or imply ordinary history is indefinitely retained. Exceptional resolution requires separately reviewed recovery/runbook; no operator mutation is authorized.
4. Each category's expiry restricts its permitted use, subject to explicit active-state/never-archived safety exceptions. Copy-bearing receipts, minimal ack/fences, incidents and backups follow their own retention. Raw coordinate deletion does not imply all identity/timestamp metadata or legitimate copies vanish.
5. Token cleanup, retention/access expiry, conditional work and backup restoration protections are future implementation requirements, not claims that source performs them. No public wire fields or direct client write grants are introduced.

After acceptance, only current participants see allowed trip/location projection; terminal state revokes live location reads immediately. Tokens/private presence/eligibility evidence/receipts/work remain private. |

Revoked owner may read exact prior success receipt and retained own state/history, but gains no new mutation authority. |

Opaque 256-bit/43-character cursor scoped to UID/role/query/fixed limit/upper bound; one-hour expiry from first page inherited by descendants. Invalid/expired cursor requires explicit refresh. Late archive may remain unseen until refresh; lastTerminal/getRide recovery stays separate. |

## Approved retention policy

All periods are elapsed UTC time from the anchor below. Expiry prevents further permitted use at the deadline even if asynchronous physical cleanup lags; physical cleanup targets require implementation/operations verification. No cleanup may clear a newer resource, reset a generation, reexecute expired IDs or recreate deleted history by old archive retries. Copy-bearing receipts/operational records follow their own category; deleting one source does not imply every copy vanished. Later policy changes require scoped review.

| ID | Category / approved retention and safety rule |
| --- | --- |
| R01 Active nonterminal business state | No automatic time-based deletion or synthetic completion. Retain until valid guarded terminal resolution. Operator reviews unresolved incidents each staffed day; prolonged cases stay explicit safety exceptions, never silently freed to satisfy a TTL. Pilot cannot start without support/recovery gate below. |
| R02 Live terminal snapshots | Retain at least 24 hours from endedAt AND until archive acknowledgement; cleanup only after both conditions. Archive failure retains live snapshot/work and raises incident rather than deleting sole recovery evidence. If still unarchived at normal history expiry, retain as unresolved exception and escalate; do not claim indefinite ordinary history retention. |
| R03 Firestore participant trip history | 90 days from endedAt, then delete through separately implemented retention cleanup. Includes coordinates/addresses within the terminal snapshot. History/getRide may legitimately return unavailable/not-found after expiry; never fabricate an old trip. Archive retry after 90 days must not resurrect expired history; safe acknowledgement/cleanup handling must be implemented and tested before its activation. |
| R04 Command receipts/results | 30 days from original committedAt; replay never extends retention. Receipt snapshots may contain trip/offer/location copies until this deadline. After pruning, embedded command age permanently prevents reexecution. Early account deletion must not make an old command executable. |
| R05 Closed offers | 24 hours from resolvedAt, excluding limited offer copies already stored within retained receipts. No active pending offer is TTL-deleted without guarded resolution. |
| R06 Role-scoped lastTerminal | Latest reference per UID/role, replaced only by a newer valid terminal event; expire 30 days from its endedAt. It never displaces newer active state and does not extend history lifetime. |
| R07 Latest raw presence/projection | No raw location history. Latest coordinate-bearing sample/projection retained only up to 30 minutes from acceptedAt, or removed sooner on session close/replacement/terminal projection cleanup as applicable. Invalid freshness already blocks use far earlier. Cleanup removes raw payload, not active business assignment, sequence high-water mark or required duplicate-ack metadata. Stale projection cannot be used as a fresh tracking position; current nonterminal participants may still read/display it as stale until cleanup. Terminal authorization revokes reads immediately. |
| R08 Publisher fences/control | UID generation high-water mark and minimal current publisher/session/control metadata remain while account exists, as required to prevent stale session revival. Keep current session lastSequence, one-way lastSampleFingerprint and original lastAck as needed for C02 exact duplicate recognition even after raw-coordinate cleanup; these contain no raw coordinate or token payload. Session replacement/close retains only metadata necessary for existing fenced replay/close behavior. Account deletion revokes authentication before fence removal. Online revision never resets while UID exists. |
| R09 Push token | Private current token retained until explicit clear/logout/account deletion or 30 days without accepted telemetry, whichever comes first; no delivery guarantee/offer dependency. Token replacement deletes prior token. No tokens in logs, history or participant projection. |
| R10 Cursor registry | Usable only until inherited first-page-issuedAt + 1 hour; then eligible for TTL deletion. Scope checks and exact expiry precede use even if physical record remains. |
| R11 Durable work | Unfinished work retained until acknowledged/safely superseded with authoritative identity checks; no TTL that loses a committed intent. Finished work metadata retained 24 hours from acknowledgement, then prune. A retry cannot recreate an effect after the effect's retention deadline. |
| R12 Application local state | Role/account cache expires after 24 hours since validation and clears on logout/account deletion; known identity needed for an unresolved command is kept as command metadata, never treated as authoritative active state. Automatic lifecycle retries stop after 24 hours; unresolved exact command metadata retained up to 30 days from command creation for reconciliation, then delete without new execution. Noncurrent user data is never rendered. Existing explicitly durable acquisition/sequence identity follows account scope; telemetry sample payload is not durable. |
| R13 Logs and support records | Structured operational logs 30 days from event; no raw coordinates, addresses, token, command payload or cursor. Minimal incident record 90 days from closure; unresolved cases reviewed each staffed day and retain only necessary support facts. Record access-controlled participant/ride references and actual operator actions without pretending an operations note changed trip state. |
| R14 Backups/exports | Operational backups/exports expire 30 days from creation, are access restricted and not exposed as rider history. A restore must reapply current deletion/expiry/auth fencing before serving traffic; do not revive expired commands/sessions or deleted records. Retention cleanup and backup deletion are future implementation/release checks. |
| R15 Exceptions/account deletion | No blanket indefinite legal-hold default. A specifically authorized preservation exception needs documented purpose/owner/scope and review every 30 days; it cannot broaden participant/API access or justify inventing a terminal state. Account deletion is a separate future guarded workflow with authentication revocation and inventory of copies; no deletion endpoint is added by this contract. |

These are approved retention decisions, not assertions of legal compliance or implemented deletion. Cleanup timing, outage backlogs, active safety exceptions and deletion of copies must be documented in the pilot privacy/operations runbook before activation.
